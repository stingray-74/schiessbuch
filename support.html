Frequently asked questions

Which rule applies to me?

Anyone who wants to acquire their own guns in Germany proves regular practice (§ 14 Weapons Act). According to the German Shooting Federation (DSB), as of 2026: in the first twelve months at least once a month or 18 times in total. At the review after five years the last 24 months count, with once per quarter or six times a year. After ten years club membership is enough. Rules differ between federations and authorities, please ask your club. The app documents, it gives no legal advice. Choose the rule under Settings › Shooter.

How does the app count range days?

Several entries on the same day count as one range day. The rule asks for sessions, not disciplines. The home screen shows whether the current month (or quarter) is done, how many months are covered and how many range days are still missing.

How do I add an entry?

Tap the plus at the bottom. Date and time are filled in, as are range, discipline, gun and number of shots from your last entry. Result, lane and note are optional. "Save and add another" keeps day, range and gun when you shoot several disciplines on one day.

How does the range officer confirm an entry?

Open the entry, tap "Get it confirmed" and hand the device to the range officer. They enter their name and sign with a finger on the screen. Alternatively take a photo of the signed entry in your paper log. Other unconfirmed entries of the same day can be confirmed at once.

Can I change a confirmed entry?

Yes, but only with a remark. Confirmed entries are locked so your proof stays credible. The remark is stored with its date and appears in the PDF as "amended".

How do I get the proof for my club or the authority?

Settings › Proof of practice as PDF. Choose the period, then share or print the PDF via the share sheet. It contains your details (name, club, membership and licence number if entered), all entries with signature or photo of the confirmation, a summary line with entries, range days and shots, and the range days per month. The current year is free, all years and custom periods come with Premium.

Which disciplines does the app know?

Common DSB and BDS disciplines in six groups: air guns, smallbore, centerfire handgun, centerfire rifle, shotgun and other. Just type your own discipline into the search of the discipline picker, it then appears under "Recently used".

What does the gun list do?

Per gun: name, type, caliber, licence number, photo and a shot counter that counts along with your entries (plus a starting value for shots before the app). With Premium you set a cleaning interval and the app shows "Cleaning due" on the home screen and in the list. "Cleaned" resets the counter. If you delete a gun, the entries are kept and keep the name.

How does the reminder work?

Under Settings turn on "Month without an entry". The app then notifies you on the 25th of a month if you have not logged a session that month. The notification is scheduled locally on the device, no server is involved.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server, even the signatures stay on the phone. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I back up my data or switch phones?

Under Settings › "Create backup" you save everything into one file, optionally encrypted with a password (recommended, because licence numbers and signatures are in it), for example to iCloud Drive or via AirDrop to your computer. On the new device choose "Restore backup".

Can I use the data in Excel?

Yes. Under Settings you export all entries and guns as an Excel file with two sheets, or the entries as CSV.

What does the app cost?

Entries are free without limit, plus two guns, confirmation by the range officer, the PDF for the current year, backup and export. No ads. Premium adds unlimited guns, cleaning reminders and the PDF for all years and custom periods. You pay yearly or once. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.

How do I delete my data?

Under Settings › "Delete all data", or by deleting the app. Since nothing is stored on servers, that removes everything.
