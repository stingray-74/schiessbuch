Privacy Policy

for the app "Schießbuch" (iOS)

HelpLegal noticeDeutsch

In short: Everything you enter in Schießbuch stays on your device. There is no user account, no cloud, no advertising and no analysis of your behaviour. We never see your entries, guns, licence numbers or the range officers' signatures. You create the PDF proof yourself on the device and only pass it on if you want to.

2. What data the app processes

The app stores the data you enter yourself: entries (date, time, range or club, discipline, gun, number of shots, result, lane, note), confirmations by the range officer (name, signature as an image or photo of the signed paper entry, time, remarks on later changes), guns (name, type, caliber, licence number, photo, counters, cleaning interval), your details for the proof (name, club, membership number, licence number) and settings (language, colour scheme, proof rule, reminder, Premium status). This data is kept exclusively in a local database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

The range officer's signature is personal data of that person. It is stored only on your device and appears solely in the PDF proof that you create and pass on yourself. Please obtain the officer's consent before they sign on your device.

Insofar as any processing by us takes place at all, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the app). Local storage on your device does not require any processing by us.

4. Purchases (Premium)

You buy Premium through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data, no name and no address. Apple's privacy policy applies.

To verify and manage purchases we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This involves a random, anonymous user identifier, the store's purchase receipts and technical details such as app version, device type and system language. This identifier is not linked to your name. RevenueCat is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract).

5. Proof, backup and export

You create the PDF proof, backups and exports (Excel, CSV) yourself. The file is only stored where you send it via the share sheet, for example iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email or a printer. Backups can be encrypted with a password in the app, which we recommend because of the licence numbers and signatures. The privacy policies of the respective providers apply to these services. We do not receive a copy.

6. No analytics, no advertising, no sharing

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising and no social media integration. We do not share data with third parties because we do not have any. The app is not legal advice: it documents your range sessions, and the notes on the rules follow the publications of the German Shooting Federation (as of 2026); your federation and authority are decisive.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You delete it in the app under Settings › "Delete all data", individual entries and guns in their detail view, or by removing the app from your device. The anonymous purchase identifier at RevenueCat is deleted according to the retention periods applicable there.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not store any personal data about you, these rights are in practice exercised on your device: access and portability via the export and backup functions, deletion as described in section 7. If you have questions, write to schiessbuch@bellmann.solutions.

9. This website

The support pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time and requested page in server logs. We use no cookies, no analytics tools and no embedded third-party content on this website. Details in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

10. Children

The app is aimed at sport shooters and not at children under 16. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

If the app's features change, for example through an optional confirmation by QR code at the club, we will update this policy and point it out in the app.
